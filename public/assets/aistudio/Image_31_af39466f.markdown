## Brand & Style

### Design Narrative
The design system embodies "Festive Editorial Precision"â€”the union of joyous Indian event hospitality with rigorous, audit-grade financial governance. It is designed for event hosts, families, and master planners who must reconcile high emotional stakes with uncompromising financial reality. Rather than relying on generic celebration tropes or cold enterprise software patterns, the interface balances warm ceremonial warmth with mathematical precision.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Editorial Neoclassicism meets Fintech Density
- **Warm Editorial Canvas:** Soft, non-glare alabaster paper-like surfaces replace stark enterprise cool grays.
- **Typographic Duality:** High-contrast editorial display serifs for ceremonial storytelling, balanced against robust geometric grotesques for workflow ergonomics, and fixed-width tabular figures for currency accounting.
- **Honest Provenance & Deterministic Feedback:** Every estimate, user entry, and algorithmic simulation is visually isolated. Interfaces never masquerade AI assumptions as final truth.
- **Asymmetric Grid Cadence:** Bento-inspired operational groupings with subtle 1px dividers, restrained depth tiers, and focused callouts for risk indicators and financial variance.

## Colors

### Hierarchy & Functional Application
- **Primary (`#3730A3` - Deep Utsav Indigo):** The executive anchor. Used for primary state actions, active navigation anchors, selected scenario boundaries, and keyboard focus indicators.
- **Secondary (`#9A3412` - Kumkum Terracotta):** Cultural grounding and cohort distinction. Used for family milestones, heritage-specific requirements, and ritual indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Festive Saffron/Marigold):** Celebratory sparks, trade-off highlights, priority indicators, and generative AI activity.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Warm Alabaster):** Organic paper-like base that prevents display fatigue during intensive planning sessions.
